About CHRIST (Deemed to be University)

CHRIST (Deemed to be University) is a NAAC A+ accredited institution with campuses across Bangalore, Delhi NCR and Pune. Guided by the vision of “Excellence and Service,” it runs 10 schools offering 250+ programmes to a student body of 38,000+, including 520+ international students from 38+ countries.
