01 — The Problem · scroll

Traditional university ERPs are *passive* databases. They *wait* to be queried. Insights hide behind manual reports, at-risk students *slip* *through*, parents stay *disconnected* — and institutions react to dropouts, burnout and shortages only *after* they happen.

OpaqueParents see results once a semester
