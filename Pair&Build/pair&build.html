About Us

CHRIST (Deemed to be University) is born out of the educational vision of St Kuriakose Elias Chavara, an educationalist and social reformer of the nineteenth century in South India. The University is committed to providing world-class education, emphasizing academic excellence and holistic development.

The MSc in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AIML) is a cutting-edge program designed to equip students with deep theoretical and practical knowledge in the fields of AI, Deep Learning, Data Science, and Cognitive Computing. Our curriculum is tailored to bridge the gap between academic research and industry demands, fostering innovators and technology leaders for the future.
